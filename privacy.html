Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

In short

  • Your snag photos never leave your phone. They're stored in the app's local storage only — Snagly does not upload photo images to any server today.
  • Text you enter — property address, room names, snag descriptions, status updates — syncs to Firebase (Google Cloud) so it can appear on other devices you or people you invite are signed in on.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or any tracking beyond what's needed to make sync and sign-in work.
  • Deleting your account removes your sign-in credential but does not currently delete your synced property data — see "Deleting your data" below.
  • Contact us any time at support@snagly.house.

Who we are

Snagly is a snagging app for UK new-build homeowners, developed by Snagly ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Snagly Android app collects, why, where it's stored, and how you can control or delete it.

Account creation and sign-in

When you first open Snagly, the app automatically creates an anonymous account for you via Firebase Authentication — this only stores a randomly generated device identifier, no personal information. You can optionally upgrade this to a permanent account using:

  • Google Sign-In — we receive the identity token Google provides (name, email address and profile photo URL associated with your Google account) to authenticate you.
  • Email and password — we store the email address, a securely hashed password (handled entirely by Firebase Authentication; we never see your plaintext password), and the display name you choose.

We use whichever identity provider you choose solely to authenticate you and, where you invite others to your property, to show them who's contributing.

What data syncs to the cloud

If you add more than one device, or invite someone to your property, the following data is stored in Cloud Firestore (Google's cloud database, part of Firebase) so it can be kept in sync:

  • Your property address and its rooms, floors and outside areas.
  • Each snag you log: description, room, category, status, dates, and any timeline notes.
  • Your display name, and the display name/email of anyone you invite to your property, along with when they joined and what they've contributed.
  • Photo metadata — how many photos are attached to a snag, and its local file path on the device that took it — but never the photo image itself. See "Photos" below.
  • In-app notifications (for example, "Jane requested photos for the kitchen tap snag") and requests to add photos to a snag.
  • Invite codes used to join a property.

This data is only visible to you and to people you've explicitly invited to your property.

Photos

Photos you attach to a snag are saved to your device's local storage only. The app has photo-upload functionality built into its code, but it is intentionally switched off — no photo image is transmitted to Snagly's servers or anywhere else. If you use Snagly on more than one device, photos taken on one device will not appear on your other devices, because nothing is uploaded. We plan to introduce optional cloud photo backup in future; if we do, this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly communicated in the app.

What stays on your device only

Some data never leaves your phone and is not part of Firestore sync:

  • Photo image files themselves (see above).
  • App settings — theme, report preferences, recent searches, and your notification/reminder preferences.
  • Locally scheduled reminder notifications.

This data lives in the app's local database and is removed if you uninstall the app.

PDF reports

When you generate a snag report, the PDF is created entirely on your device using the data already stored there. Nothing is sent to a server to produce the report. Once created, the file is yours to share by whatever method you choose (email, messaging apps, print, etc.) — we have no visibility into how you share it.

Sharing your property with others

If you invite someone to your property (or accept an invite), everyone with access can see each member's display name, email address (if provided), when they joined, their access level, and what they've added to the snag list. If a member is later removed from a property, their name remains attached to the snags they previously added or updated — removing access doesn't retroactively anonymise past contributions.

Notifications

Snagly can show local notifications on your device — for example, a reminder before a scheduled fix date, or an alert that someone has requested photos for a snag. These are scheduled and shown entirely on your device; we do not operate a push-notification server today. The app requests the Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission to show these, and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to re-schedule reminders after your phone restarts.

Device permissions

  • Camera — to let you photograph a snag directly from the app.
  • Notifications — to show local reminders and alerts (see above).
  • Boot / restart — to re-schedule your local reminders if your phone restarts.

Snagly does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or call history.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from Settings within the app. Doing so removes your Firebase sign-in credential and signs you back in anonymously. At present, this does not automatically delete your synced property, snag, or share data from our servers, or the local data stored on your device. If you'd like your cloud data fully deleted, email support@snagly.house from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it manually. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored locally on that device, including your photos.

Children's privacy

Snagly is intended for adults managing a new-build home and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data retention

We retain your synced data for as long as your account and property exist, so the app continues to work across your devices and with anyone you've invited. See "Deleting your data" above for how to request removal.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

If you're in the UK or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for anything else, contact us at support@snagly.house.

Changes to this policy

If we change what data Snagly collects or how it's used — for example, if we introduce cloud photo backup — we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be called out in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: support@snagly.house.
