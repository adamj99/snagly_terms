How it works

1

Set up the house

Add your floors, rooms and outside areas. Snagly suggests a starting list so a four-bed takes a couple of minutes.

2

Walk it room by room

Photograph each snag, pick a category and a priority, add a line of description. It all lands in the right room.

3

Send it and track it

Export a PDF for your builder, then move each snag from open to reported, in progress and resolved as the fixes happen.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone

Photos of the inside of your home are never uploaded to Snagly. When someone you've shared with asks for them, you get a notification and send them yourself, by message or email, from your own phone.

The rest of a shared property — the rooms, descriptions and dates — syncs so both phones show the same thing. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

What is snagging?

Snagging is checking a newly built home for defects, from paint drips and gaps in sealant to doors that don't close. New-build warranties usually give you a period to report them to the builder, who fixes them at no cost.

How much does it cost?

Snagly is free to download and use. A one-off in-app purchase unlocks the full app. There's no subscription.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Snagly is on Android through Google Play.

Why can't the person I share with see my photos?

Photos are never uploaded, so they only exist on the phone that took them. The person you share with can ask for them, and you send them from your phone however you like.

Do I need an account?

Snagly signs you in automatically when you open it, so there's nothing to set up first. You can add an email or Google sign-in later, which is worth doing before you share a property or switch phones, so you don't lose access.

Can I use it for more than one property?

Yes. Each property has its own rooms, snags and sharing.
