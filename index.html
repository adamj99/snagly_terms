What it does

Everything you need to get your new-build fixed

No spreadsheets, no lost emails, no "which photo was that again." Snagly keeps the whole snag list — and your builder's progress on it — in one place.

Dashboard

Every snag, in one place

Log it, photograph it, chase it until it's fixed. See open, reported, in-progress and resolved snags at a glance, with the count that matters most front and centre.

Add a snag

Add a snag in seconds

Room, category, photos, done. The categories you actually need come first, and your most recent room is one tap away when you're logging several snags in the same spot.

Room grid

Every room at a glance

Six rooms or thirty, one screen — no scrolling to find the problem one. Grouped by floor, alphabetised, and colour-coded by how many snags still need attention.

Analytics

See where the problems cluster

By category and by room, plus how quickly your builder actually responds. Useful the day you sit down with your site manager and need the evidence, not just the anecdote.

PDF report

Send your builder a clean PDF

Cover page, grouping, photos and dates — generated on your phone, with a box for the site manager to hand-write a scheduled fix date. No printer required on your end.

Outside areas

Inside and out

Gardens, drive, roof and garage are covered by the same warranty. Track them alongside every internal room instead of juggling a separate list.

Snag detail

Photos, status, full history

Every update logged against the snag, so nothing gets forgotten or disputed. Invite your partner or family to add snags too — every contribution stays credited to them.
